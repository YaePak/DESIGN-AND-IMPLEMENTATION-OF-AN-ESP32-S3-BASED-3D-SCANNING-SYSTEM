## TASKS FOR THIS WEEK

### 1. Adjustment and Improvement of the Mechanical Frame

- Inspect and adjust the mechanical frame to ensure that it is suitable for the object scanning process.
- Optimize the mounting positions of components and mechanical parts to ensure system stability during operation.
- Check the dimensions, structural strength, and movement of the mechanical mechanisms.
- Prepare the mechanical frame for system assembly and 3D scanning tests.

### 2. Component Testing and PCB Soldering

- Inspect the condition and specifications of the components before assembly.
- Test the functionality of the components to identify any defective or unsuitable components.
- Solder the components onto the PCB according to the circuit design.
- Inspect the quality of the solder joints and the connections between the components and the PCB after soldering.
- Perform an initial inspection and functional test of the PCB to ensure that it operates properly and is ready for system integration.

### Objectives

Complete and optimize the mechanical frame to meet the requirements of object scanning, while completing component testing and PCB assembly to prepare for the system integration, operation, and testing phase.
